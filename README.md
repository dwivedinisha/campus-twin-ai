\# CampusTwin AI



Agentic AI-Driven Digital Twin for Autonomous Smart Campus Resource Optimization.



A simulation-first digital twin platform for a virtual smart campus (2 buildings, 10 rooms),

covering real-time monitoring, ML prediction, what-if simulation, AI recommendations,

and human-approved simulated actuation.
